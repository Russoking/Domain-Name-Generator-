Privacy Policy

What this site does with your data

The domain name generator, and every tool linked from this site, runs entirely inside your browser. When you type a word or select a style, nothing is sent to our servers — the generator logic executes locally on your device, and no form data, generated results, or usage details are collected, stored, or transmitted by us.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

Analytics

This site does not currently use any third-party analytics or tracking scripts beyond the advertising cookies described above.

Third-party links

Some tools on this site link out to third-party registrars (such as Namecheap) to let you check domain availability. Once you leave this site, that third party's own privacy policy applies to any information you provide there — we have no control over and are not responsible for their practices.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
